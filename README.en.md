# Micélio — The City in a Loop

🇧🇷 [Português](README.md) · 🇺🇸 **English**

A 2D pixel-art metroidvania that runs right in the browser. A single file, nothing to install.

**▶ Play:** https://luucascavalheiro.github.io/micelio/

> The game's text is currently in Portuguese.

## Story

The city wakes up at 6:00. Always at 6:00. In the hospital basement, a giant fungus holds time in a loop. Every death restarts the day, but your weapons and your strength remain. Cross streets, houses and buildings, reach the city center and destroy the Primordial Fungus to break the loop.

## Controls

| Action | Key |
|---|---|
| Move | A / D or ← / → |
| Jump | Space or W |
| Drop through a platform | S + jump |
| Attack | J, X or click |
| Switch weapon | Q or 1–9 |
| Use medkit | H |
| Open chest / door | E |
| Pause (arsenal and objective) | P |
| Sound | M |

Click the screen before playing so the keyboard works.

## The world

Outskirts → Abandoned House → Street of the Dead → Apartment Building → Central Square → Santa Aurora Hospital → Basement. The Sewer is an optional area.

- A barricade on the Street of the Dead can only be cleared with the double jump, found at the top of the building.
- The hospital only opens with the access card carried by the Colossus, the miniboss in the square.
- There are three hidden treasures, two of them behind cracked walls that break when attacked.

## Systems

- **Weapons:** Knife, Ritual Dagger, 9mm Pistol, Shotgun, Submachine Gun, Fire Axe, .44 Magnum, Assault Rifle, Combat Scythe and Lunar Scythe. They come from chests or monster drops.
- **Infinite ammo** for firearms.
- **Per-weapon levels:** each weapon earns its own XP and gains +12% damage per level.
- **Health bar only:** no mana. Medkits are scarce and must be rationed.
- **Time loop:** when you die, you return to the last anchor clock with the same levels, weapons and items. Enemies respawn.
- **Progress is saved** in the browser.

## Monsters

Zombie, Rotten Hound, Crawler, Spitter, Hunter, Spore, the Colossus miniboss and the final boss, the Primordial Fungus, which has two phases.

## Run locally

Download the repository and open `index.html` in a browser.
